My Baby

Let's set up your crib sensors. You'll plug them into this computer once, then they work wirelessly.
1 Your home WiFi
The sensors need to join your WiFi to send data to this computer. Use a 2.4 GHz network — the sensors can't see 5 GHz-only networks.
2 Connect & configure sensors
Plug both sensors into this computer with USB cables.
3 Start monitoring
Place sensors on the crib rails. Your baby's vitals will appear on screen.
Not a medical device. My Baby is a sleep-insight tool for curious parents. It cannot detect health emergencies and must never replace safe-sleep practices or adult supervision.
